## I. What is this project ?
- This is a strategic roguelite autobattler game I made during Haven game jam of Hack Club with my buddy. This game was made in Godot game engine.

## II. Features:
- An interesting autobattler mechanic: Animals bounce around in the battlefield like the DVD ScreenSaver. And they have different unique interactions when they bounce against other animals.
- A diverse range of animals to build your army and strategy:
    - Crab: Can only moves horizontally and deals damage to enemy on touch
    - Chick: Grow after 20 seconds. After growing, it shoots an egg when touching with the edges, which heal allies and damages enemies
    - Wolf: Becomes hungry after 30 seconds. When hungry, it loses HP and devours the first enemy it touches and gain their HP.
    - Turtle: A slow and tanky unit that deals damage to enemy on touch.
    - Pig: Leave a pie of poo when touching the edge. Enemies stepping on the poo will loses HP and gets stunned.
    - Porcupine: On contact with enemies, launch spikes in all direction, dealing damage to anything touches.
- A roguelite system with strategic depth, allowing players to see the enemies they will face, thus letting them choose an animal out of the 3 choices given and add to their team before every battle.
<img width="1150" height="647" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/302e71ac-9d60-4ecc-bcbc-a4ce591babc7" />

- A strategic mechanic allowing players to set up the position and initial direction of their animal before combat. Allowing players to allow their offensive units to be aggresive and conserve their late-game units, or ball and let the chaos unfold.
<img width="1157" height="647" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6e978cc2-09fc-4cf4-a0a6-faf96fb8f8df" />

- A battle timer that starts at the start of combat. When it ticks down to zero, a sandstorm appears and deal damage to every standing animals, to settle down long matches. And perhaps, benefitting the tankier animals ?
- Sets of buttons allow players to choose any game speed, to watch the battle at normal speed or speed up to the results (Speeding up may leads to some bug)

## III. Controls
- Space key to start every battle
- Drag and drop animals to set up their positions
- Shift key while dragging to set the direction for the animals

# IV. AI Disclosure
- AI was used to ask about Godot built-in functions: draw shapes functions, types of nodes in scene trees and signal system as we are kind of beginners to Godot game engine.
